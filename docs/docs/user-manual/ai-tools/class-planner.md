# Class Planner

The Class Planner is an AI-powered tool designed to help teachers create comprehensive lesson plans quickly and efficiently. By leveraging artificial intelligence, it generates detailed lesson plans based on your specific requirements and teaching materials.

## Features

- **Subject and Theme-Based Planning**: Create lesson plans tailored to specific subjects and themes
- **Customizable Lesson Count**: Generate plans for single or multiple connected lessons
- **Playfulness Adjustment**: Control the balance between structured and playful activities
- **Material Integration**: Upload your existing teaching materials to inform the AI
- **Multiple AI Models**: Choose from various AI models including Llama 3.2 and GPT models
- **Classroom Integration**: Connect plans directly to your classroom
- **Export Options**: Download plans as PDF or copy to clipboard

## How to Use

1. **Enter Basic Information**: Specify the subject, theme, and number of lessons
2. **Select Your Classroom**: Choose which classroom the plan is for
3. **Adjust Playfulness Level**: Set how structured or playful you want the activities to be
4. **Upload Materials (Optional)**: Add existing documents to help inform the AI
5. **Add Additional Information**: Include any specific requirements or notes
6. **Generate Plan**: Click the button to create your lesson plan
7. **Review and Export**: View the generated plan and save it in your preferred format

## Benefits

- **Save Time**: Create complete lesson plans in minutes instead of hours
- **Enhance Creativity**: Get fresh ideas and approaches for teaching familiar subjects
- **Maintain Consistency**: Ensure your lessons follow a coherent structure
- **Customize for Your Students**: Tailor plans to match your classroom's specific needs
- **Integrate with Your Materials**: Build upon your existing teaching resources

The Class Planner tool helps teachers focus more on delivering quality education by reducing the time spent on administrative planning tasks.
